Xiaomi devices aggressively kill background services by default. Follow these steps to keep HyperBridge running smoothly:

{{< steps >}}

### Enable Autostart
Go to **Security app** > **Manage apps** > **HyperBridge** > Toggle **Autostart** to **Allowed**.

### Set Battery Saver to No Restrictions
In the same app info menu, tap **Battery saver** and select **No restrictions**.

### Lock in Recents (Optional)
Open the app switcher, press and hold the **HyperBridge** card, and tap the **Lock icon**.

{{< /steps >}}
