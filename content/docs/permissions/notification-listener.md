HyperBridge needs access to system notifications to detect active music, downloads, timers, and messages.

## Privacy & Security
HyperBridge processes notification data **100% locally in ephemeral memory**. No notification contents are ever logged, sent to servers, or shared with third parties.

## How to Enable

1. Open **Settings** > **Apps** > **Special app access** > **Notification access**.
2. Locate **HyperBridge** in the list.
3. Toggle the switch to **Allow**.
4. Confirm the system security prompt.
