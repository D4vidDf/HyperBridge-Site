After installing HyperBridge, follow these quick steps to get your first Dynamic Island running.

---

### Step 1: Launch the App

Open **HyperBridge** from your app launcher. You will be greeted by the Material 3 Expressive Welcome screen.

---

### Step 2: Run Setup Health Checks

HyperBridge automatically tests whether your device supports Super Island APIs and highlights any missing permissions.

---

### Step 3: Grant Notification Listener Access

Tap **Grant Permission** to allow HyperBridge to read notifications locally on your device. HyperBridge requires this permission to convert incoming notifications into dynamic island popups.

---

### Step 4: Enable the applications

Select the apps you want Hyper Bridge to show in the Super Island.
