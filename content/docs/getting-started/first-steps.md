After installing HyperBridge, follow these quick steps to get your first Dynamic Island running.

{{< steps >}}

### Launch the App
Open **HyperBridge** from your app launcher. You will be greeted by the Material 3 Expressive Welcome screen.

### Run Setup Health Checks
HyperBridge tests whether your device supports Super Island APIs and highlights any missing permissions.

### Grant Notification Listener Access
Tap **Grant Permission** to allow HyperBridge to read notifications locally on your device.

### Send a Test Notification
From the app's settings or test screen, trigger a test island (Media, Timer, or Download) to verify that the Super Island renders smoothly around your camera cutout.
