After completing the initial setup, you can control precisely which applications are bridged into dynamic islands. The **Active Apps** management screen provides fine-grained controls, category filters, and automatic rules for new installations.

---

## 1. What's New & Release Highlights

When Hyper Bridge updates or opens after setup, the What's New dialog highlights newly added features, system updates, and architecture improvements.

{{< figure

- Tap **View Changelog** to read full release notes and new translator updates online.
- Tap **Awesome!** to enter the Active Apps dashboard.

## 2. Searching and Filtering Applications

Hyper Bridge organizes your apps and system services with responsive filter chips and real-time search.

{{< figure 
    src="/img/docs/apps/en/02-active-apps-filters.jpg" 
    alt="Active Apps Filter Chips and Search" 
    caption="Filter by category: System, All, Music, Downloads, Navigation, and more." 
>}}

- **Instant Search**: Type the name or package ID of any installed app in the top search bar.
- **Category Chips**:
  - **System**: Built-in system services such as VPN, Screen Recording, and System Update.
  - **All**: Displays every installed application on your device.
  - **Music**: Filters media players (Spotify, YouTube Music, Tidal, etc.).
  - **Chats / General**: Filters instant messengers and communication tools.
- **Quick Sort**: Tap the arrow button next to the search bar to toggle ascending or descending sorting.

---

## 3. App Toggles and Configuration

Each application has an independent toggle switch and an individual settings shortcut.

{{< figure 
    src="/img/docs/apps/en/03-apps-list-toggles.jpg" 
    alt="App List with Toggles and Settings Cog" 
    caption="Enable or disable apps individually and access per-app configuration." 
>}}

- **Enable / Disable Toggle**: Switch the toggle on the right side of any app or system service to allow or block dynamic island popups for that specific app.
- **Gear Icon (Settings)**: Tap the gear icon next to any app or system service to open its specialized configuration (such as custom side icons, countdown timers, or custom translators).
- **Subtitles**: Displays current status (`Enabled` / `Disabled`) or the Android package name.

---

## 4. Enabling All Apps & Auto-Adding New Apps

If you want every installed app and all future apps to immediately display dynamic islands without manual toggling:

{{< figure 
    src="/img/docs/apps/en/04-enable-all-apps-sheet.jpg" 
    alt="Enable All Apps Bottom Sheet" 
    caption="Enable all apps at once and configure automatic bridging for future app installs." 
>}}

1. Tap the **Enable all apps** card at the top of the list.
2. Toggle the main switch to enable Hyper Island for all apps in the current list simultaneously.
3. Tap the **Settings icon** on the "Enable all apps" item to open the bottom sheet modal:
   - **Add new apps automatically**: When toggled on, whenever you install a new app from Google Play or an APK, Hyper Bridge will automatically enable dynamic island bridging for it without requiring you to return to settings.
