HyperBridge supports over 20 languages thanks to our amazing community translators!

## How to Help Translate

1. Visit our [Crowdin Project](https://crowdin.com/project/hyper-bridge).
2. Create a free account or sign in with GitHub.
3. Select your language and start translating strings.
4. Approved translations are automatically synchronized into the next app update!
