## Overview

**Silksong: Sea of Sorrow** is a dark Material 3 color scheme (`.hscheme`) crafted for HyperBridge Studio widgets and island themes, inspired by the atmospheric palette of *Hollow Knight: Silksong – Sea of Sorrow*.

### Core Extracted Palette

- **Primary (`#87DAE4`)**: Bioluminescent sea cyan used for active progress bars, primary buttons, and glowing island accents.
- **Secondary (`#E8B96E`)**: Gilded needle and lantern brass for secondary actions, metadata badges, and subtle highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#FB4E47`)**: Hornet's signature crimson cloak for live status indicators, alerts, and high-contrast tags.
- **Surface (`#001B17`)**: Deep abyssal sea-trench teal base paired with stepped tonal containers (`#04241F`, `#0D332D`, `#001310`) for rich depth without harsh pure black.

### How to Import in HyperBridge

1. Tap **Download (`.hscheme`)** to save `silksong_sea_of_sorrow.hscheme` to your device.
2. Open **HyperBridge → Studio → Global → Color Scheme** and tap **Import Palette**, or open the downloaded `.hscheme` file directly with HyperBridge.
