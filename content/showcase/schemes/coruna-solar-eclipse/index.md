## Overview

**La Coruña Total Eclipse (12 Aug)** is an atmospheric dark Material 3 color scheme (`.hscheme`) inspired by the total solar eclipse of August 12 experienced in La Coruña. It captures the dramatic contrast between the deep totality umbra sky and the glowing amber-gold solar corona along the Atlantic horizon.

### Core Extracted Palette

- **Primary (`#D25C24`)**: Intense chromosphere and sunset horizon ember orange for primary buttons, active progress bars, and island highlights.
- **Secondary (`#FFB055`)**: Radiant solar corona and diamond-ring gold for secondary controls, badges, and luminous accents.
- **Surface (`#080302`)**: Deep totality umbra black base paired with warm shadow containers (`#140A07`, `#21120D`, `#040101`) that blend seamlessly with AMOLED status bars.

### How to Import in HyperBridge

1. Tap **Download (`.hscheme`)** to save `coruna_solar_eclipse.hscheme` to your device.
2. Open **HyperBridge → Studio → Global → Color Scheme** and tap **Import Palette**, or open the downloaded `.hscheme` file directly with HyperBridge.
