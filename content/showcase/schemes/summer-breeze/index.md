## Overview

**Summer Breeze** is a refreshing, coastal Material 3 color scheme (`.hscheme`) for HyperBridge Studio widgets and island themes. It combines luminous **Frosted Blue** (`#A8DADC`) and **Cerulean** (`#457B9D`) tones with vibrant **Punch Red** (`#E63946`) accents and crisp **Honeydew** (`#F1FAEE`) text over a deep **Oxford Navy** (`#1D3557`) surface.

### Core Palette

- **Punch Red (`#E63946`)**: High-energy coral red for secondary action buttons, live badges, and status alerts.
- **Honeydew (`#F1FAEE`)**: Soft, high-contrast off-white with a hint of mint for primary titles and readable typography.
- **Frosted Blue (`#A8DADC`)**: Airy sea-glass cyan for primary progress bars, active highlights, and secondary metadata.
- **Cerulean (`#457B9D`)**: Balanced mid-tone ocean blue for primary containers, borders, and tertiary accents.
- **Oxford Navy (`#1D3557`)**: Deep maritime navy base surface paired with stepped tonal containers (`#162944`, `#243E63`, `#101E33`).

### How to Import in HyperBridge

1. Tap **Download (`.hscheme`)** to save `summer_breeze.hscheme` to your device.
2. Open **HyperBridge → Studio → Global → Color Scheme** and tap **Import Palette**, or open the downloaded `.hscheme` file directly with HyperBridge.
