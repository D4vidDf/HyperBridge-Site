## Overview

**Neon Cyberpunk** brings electric `#00FFDD` cyan and `#FF0099` magenta accents to your Super Island, paired with expressive Material 3 `cookie` geometric icon shapes and per-app overrides for WhatsApp and Spotify.

## Highlights

- **Expressive Cookie Icon Mask (`cookie`)**: Gives app icons and caller avatars a distinctive multi-lobed Material 3 silhouette.
- **Neon Progress Meters**: Active downloads and delivery bars glow in `#FF0099` and transition to `#34C759` upon completion.
- **App-Specific Overrides**: Includes a dedicated `com.whatsapp` override with `#25D366` tinted quick-reply chips.
